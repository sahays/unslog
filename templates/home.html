Daily workflow

in order
  1. 1

    Companies

    Each target company has a research packet, a question bank, and its own sessions.

    Create a company, paste the JD + links into the research panel, then generate a tailored question bank.

    Open Companies →
  2. 2

    Stories

    Build STAR+ stories one competency at a time, prompted by the story coach.

    Do this before practice — the bullet summaries become the answers you actually deliver under pressure.

    Open Stories →
  3. 3

    Practice

    Cross-company mock interview. Pick a role and which question banks to draw from.

    The curator picks 4–6 questions tailored to your recent recurring weaknesses. Answer by voice; get a critique per turn.

    Open Practice →
  4. 4

    Journal

    Free-form notes on past experiences. No AI on this side.

    Capture raw material here whenever something happens at work — then mine it later when building stories.

    Open Journal →
